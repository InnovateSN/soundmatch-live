# SoundMatch Live — Buyer Outreach Scripts

## Core offer

SoundMatch Live is a ready-to-adapt EPK, artist discovery and booking request platform for music schools, venues, promoters, festivals and artist networks.

The goal is to sell a paid pilot, white-label licence, partnership or asset sale.

## Primary pitch line

I have built a working prototype of a live music EPK and booking request platform that can be adapted for your organisation, students, artists, venues or promoter network.

## One-line buyer framing

### Music schools

Give your students and alumni a professional EPK and booking network that helps them present themselves, receive opportunities and understand real-world music industry workflows.

### Venues

Build a structured talent discovery and booking request system instead of relying on scattered Instagram DMs, emails and spreadsheets.

### Promoters / festivals

Shortlist artists, compare EPKs and manage booking requests in one place.

### Artist service platforms

Add booking, EPK and discovery functionality to your existing artist product stack without building from zero.

### Booking agencies / managers

Present your roster through professional profiles and manage inbound booking interest in a structured way.

## Demo call structure

1. Show landing page
2. Explain the problem: scattered music booking workflow
3. Show artist discovery card
4. Show public EPK/profile
5. Show booking request
6. Show inbox/dashboard
7. Explain how it can be branded/adapted for their organisation
8. Offer pilot

## Demo close

The simplest next step would be a small pilot with your own branding and a limited set of profiles. That would let us test whether your artists/students/promoters actually use it before committing to anything larger.

## Pricing anchor

Do not start too low unless the buyer is tiny.

Suggested anchors:

- Small pilot: £1,500–£3,000
- Strong pilot: £3,000–£10,000
- White-label setup: £5,000–£25,000
- Monthly maintenance: £300–£2,000+

## Important rule

Do not explain every feature. Sell the result:

- better artist presentation
- cleaner booking requests
- less admin chaos
- better talent discovery
- employability / ecosystem value
- ready-made system instead of building from scratch
